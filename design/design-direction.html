WhyShield — Design Direction

Current look is Tailwind-default dark mode: competent, anonymous. The proposed direction pulls palette, outline weight, and motifs from the Watcher sticker sheet so a screenshot of WhyShield reads as WhyShield. Same layouts, same copy, same buttons — only the visual identity changes. Dashed boxes mark where mascot art goes.

05 Motif library

Geometric, drawn from scratch, usable without any character art. These carry the identity even in places where a mascot would be too much (Gmail banner, toolbar badge, favicon).

THE XBlocked / not what it claims. Watermark on malicious warnings. Could be the toolbar badge when a page is flagged.
THE CRYSTALAnalyzing / thinking. Loading state in the side panel. Favicon candidate. Brand accent color source.
THE CRACK"This isn't what it appears to be." Small treatment beside headlines on spoof warnings. Never on safe states.
THE TWO ORBSGold = suspicious, crimson = malicious. Abstract stand-in for the calm-eye / red-eye duality where the mascot itself won't fit.
THE OUTLINE3px border + flat offset shadow on every card. The sticker-sheet feel, translated to UI. No blur shadows anywhere.
THE BAND6px verdict stripe at the top of cards. Crimson for malicious, gold for suspicious. Replaces the ⚠ as the first thing the eye reads.

06 Notes for the implementation session

What changes, what doesn't

  1. Layouts and copy stay exactly as they are. This is a reskin, not a redesign of the UX. Every button, every hint, every tone-rule-compliant sentence survives.
  2. Palette moves to CSS custom properties in one shared file (extension/src/shared/theme.css) imported by warning, side panel, and options. Today the three surfaces each hard-code slate values.
  3. Fonts: ship one display face (Space Grotesk or similar) and one text face bundled as woff2 in public/fonts/ — no Google Fonts at runtime, extensions should never phone home for assets. Fall back to system fonts.
  4. Mascot slots are fixed-size boxes. Hand-out pose: 96×96 on the warning card. Calm face: 44×44 in side panel and options headers. Art is dropped in as PNG with transparent background; CSS doesn't change when the art arrives.
  5. The X watermark and crack only render when verdict === "malicious". Suspicious gets the gold band and no crack. Safe states never show either.
  6. Gmail banner is the exception: it lives inside Gmail's UI, so it gets the palette and the band but no mascot, no watermark, lighter border. Restraint there.
  7. Contrast check every pairing. Crimson on ink, gold on ink, muted lavender on surface — all need to clear WCAG AA. The gold especially; it may need a slightly darker variant for small text.
  8. Toolbar icon becomes the crystal on ink, 128px, with the X overlaid when a page is flagged (badge state).
